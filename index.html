How We Deliver

From first conversation to confident launch.

A clear five-stage process turns your idea into a reliable product—with visible progress, focused collaboration, and no unnecessary surprises.

01. STAGE

Discover

We align on core business objectives, define user journeys, assess technical constraints, and agree on an unambiguous functional scope.

Key Deliverables

Architecture blueprint, user stories, technical feasibility matrix & sprint roadmap.

Your Involvement

Collaborative discovery sessions and scope confirmation.

02. STAGE

Design

We translate technical requirements into clean, accessible design systems and interactive UI prototypes tested for intuitive flows.

Key Deliverables

Interactive design mockups, responsive component system & clickable user flows.

Your Involvement

UX feedback rounds and visual approval sign-off.

03. STAGE

Develop

We build your application in bi-weekly iterative sprints using modular, typed, and maintainable frontend and backend architectures.

Key Deliverables

Weekly deployed test builds, clean Git repositories, and automated CI pipelines.

Your Involvement

Bi-weekly sprint demos and test environment reviews.

04. STAGE

Test & Launch

Rigorous end-to-end testing, security audits, mobile viewport validations, and seamless zero-downtime production deployment.

Key Deliverables

Test coverage reports, production infrastructure setup, and launch checklist.

Your Involvement

Final User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and launch authorization.

05. STAGE

Support

Continuous application monitoring, performance optimization, proactive security patches, and incremental feature development.

Key Deliverables

Uptime telemetry logs, scheduled dependency updates & roadmap iterations.

Your Involvement

Monthly priority syncs and direct communication access.

About Tecklumo

A dedicated team for ambitious digital products.

Tecklumo is an independent software development team headquartered in Dhaka, Bangladesh. We build high-quality digital products for international businesses, with a particular focus on clients in Germany and broader European markets.

We don’t use generic agency buzzwords or inflated claims. Our strength lies in technical competence, meticulous attention to detail, fluent professional English communication, and a disciplined approach to building software that works as specified.

Whether you need to build a prototype from scratch, modernize an outdated legacy platform, or add specialized full-stack engineers to your existing roadmap, we provide transparent and dependable engineering services.
